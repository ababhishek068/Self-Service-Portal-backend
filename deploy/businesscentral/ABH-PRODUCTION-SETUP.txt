ABH BUSINESS CENTRAL PRODUCTION SETUP — APP 1.0.3.199
====================================================

This file contains configuration only. Do not invent users, approval limits,
stores, projects, dimensions, leave types, or benefit balances. Select the
actual ABH records already approved by Finance/HR management.

1. PUBLISH THE PAIRED EXTENSION
===============================

Publish, sync, data-upgrade, and restart BC240 in that order. Confirm installed
version 1.0.3.199 before starting the portal.

2. APPROVAL USERS
=================

Business Central Search: Approval User Setup

- Confirm every portal requester has a Business Central user mapping.
- Set Approver ID to the actual ABH approver and maintain genuine limits.
- Confirm each approver can open Approval Entries and act on assigned entries.
- Do not give an administrator ownership of another user's approval entry.

3. PURCHASE REQUISITION WORKFLOW
================================

Business Central Search: Workflows

- Import Workflows\Purchase-Requisition-Approval-Workflow.xml.
- Open PURCHASE-REQ-WF.
- In the Create Approval Requests response, select the actual ABH approval
  method/group for Department or Division Head followed by Finance/Admin.
- Enable the workflow.
- Test approve, reject, cancel, and final release using real test approvers.

After final approval, authorized teams continue the same Purchase Request:
- Operations/Store records the stock decision.
- Procurement records LPR, RFQ, evaluation, PO, delivery, and corrections.
- Finance/Admin records the required approvals, invoice, and payment.
- Operations/Store records the GRN.
- Auditor completes the post-payment review or returns it for correction.

Do not create a separate Foreign Purchase Request menu. Currency is a field on
the same Purchase Request. Assign portal/BC permissions only to the real role
holders and test that ordinary requesters cannot update these process stages.

Business Central master data used by the portal:
- Departments / Global Dimension 2 values
- Cost Center or Project codes
- Locations, Items, Fixed Assets, Units of Measure, Currencies, Suppliers

The portal requires Department, Cost Center/Project, Required Date, Purpose,
and complete line details. Technical requirements and Scope of Work/TOR remain
optional as shown in the approved form.

Use different number-series prefixes for Purchase Requests and Store
Requisitions (for example PR and SR). Do not allow the two document types to
generate the same request number because their process history is keyed by the
Business Central request number.

4. STORE REQUISITION WORKFLOW
=============================

Business Central Search: Workflow User Groups

Create/select the actual ABH group and sequence the approved roles:
1 Supervisor, 2 Department Head, 3 Division Head, 4 Finance/Admin.

For a requester who already occupies one of those roles, configure the workflow
condition/group assigned to that requester so approval starts at the next role:
- Supervisor requester: Department Head
- Department Head requester: Division Head
- Division Head requester: Finance/Admin

Then Search: Workflows

- Import Workflows\Store-Requisition-Approval-Workflow.xml.
- Open STORE-REQ-WF and select the applicable actual workflow user group in the
  Create Approval Requests response.
- Enable and test each requester-role route; do not assume the skip without a
  test Approval Entry showing the next intended approver.

Business Central master data used by the portal:
- Issuing Stores / Locations
- Items, Fixed Assets, Units of Measure

5. EMPLOYEE EXIT
================

Business Central Search: Portal Employee Transfer Setup

- Immediate Supervisor User ID is the last-resort fallback. The requester's
  Approval User Setup Approver ID and Employee Card Supervisor/HOD are used
  first, so each employee follows the correct reporting line.
- HR Approver User ID is an optional authoritative override. When populated,
  Transfer and Resignation always use that actual ABH HR approver.
- When HR Approver User ID is blank, the extension resolves one unambiguous,
  active HR owner from the ABH HR department/HOD data. If there is no eligible
  HR owner or more than one equally eligible owner, submission stops safely;
  select the intended HR Approver User ID instead of guessing.

Business Central Search: Employee Exit Requests

- Transfer and Resignation: the assigned Supervisor approves/rejects first,
  then the resolved HR approver completes the second decision.
- Employee Exit Form: recorded directly for HR information, immediately marked
  Completed, and does not create an approval request.
- Do not mark Transfer or Resignation Completed before the real HR/offboarding
  process is complete.

Production route test:

- Submit one Transfer and one Resignation for an employee with a real reporting
  line. Confirm the first Approval Entry belongs to that employee's supervisor.
- Approve as the supervisor and confirm the next Approval Entry belongs to the
  configured/resolved HR user—not the requester or the same supervisor.
- Submit an Employee Exit Form and confirm it is Completed with no Approval Entry.

The CuPortalEmployeeExit SOAP codeunit is published automatically during
extension install/upgrade. Search Web Services and confirm it is published if
the Employee Exit page cannot connect.

6. HOD FUNCTIONS
================

The portal grants HOD access from Business Central department ownership. For
each real department head:

- Confirm the employee has the correct Global Dimension 2 / Department code.
- Confirm the employee is maintained as the Staff No. on the corresponding
  DEPARTMENTS dimension value used by QyDimensionValues.
- Sign out and sign in again after changing department ownership or employee
  organisation data. Portal roles are rebuilt at login.
- Test Department Staff and Staff on Leave with two departments. An HOD must
  see only employees in their own department, and Staff on Leave must include
  only leave that is active for the selected day.

HOD_OVERRIDE_EMPNOS may be used only for a formally authorized HOD whose
Business Central ownership data cannot yet be corrected. Do not enable
HOD_GRANT_ALL_AUTHENTICATED in production.

7. HR POLICIES & FORMS
======================

Business Central Search: Human Resources Setup

- Configure HR Document Nos with the approved number series. Upload fails
  safely when this number series is missing; do not bypass the setup.

Business Central Search: Web Services

- Publish page Hr Document Downloads as service name PgHrDownloads.
- Publish query Document Attachments (object 50108) as QyDocumentAttachments.
- Confirm the existing StaffPortal codeunit remains published as StaffPortal.

The portal stores each uploaded file as a Business Central Document Attachment
against table 51007 (Hr Document Downloads). PDF and Microsoft Office files up
to 10 MB are accepted. Upload and permanent delete are authorized by the portal
backend only for employees whose Business Central department is configured in
HR_DEPARTMENT_CODES, whose job title is HR/Human Resources, or whose employee
number is explicitly listed in HR_OVERRIDE_EMPNOS. Ordinary staff can list and
download published documents only; unpublished HR drafts remain hidden.

Production access test:

- Sign in as an authorized HR employee and upload one unpublished draft. It
  must be visible only to HR.
- Publish a controlled test document and confirm an ordinary employee can
  download it but sees no Upload or Delete control.
- Verify a direct ordinary-staff POST or DELETE request returns HTTP 403.
- Delete the controlled test document as HR and confirm both its header and
  attachment are removed from Business Central.

Use HR_OVERRIDE_EMPNOS only for approved exceptions. Keep the backend service
credential private: frontend role visibility is not the security boundary.

8. ATTACHMENTS
==============

- Staff Claim: at least one Document Attachment is mandatory before Request
  Approval in both the portal and Business Central.
- Sick/Medical leave: at least one Document Attachment is mandatory before
  Request Approval in both the portal and Business Central.
- The rule does not weaken any existing medical-benefit or leave validation.

9. ATTENDANCE
=============

Policy enforced by the paired app/portal:
- Normal arrival 08:30; arrival through 08:50 is within grace.
- Normal departure 17:30; departure from 17:20 is within grace.
- Open attendance sessions are closed at 19:00.
- A portal warning appears after the configured weekly late threshold (default 3).

The portal backend must run continuously and the Windows server local time/time
zone must be correct. The packaged startup-task helper can keep it running.

11. STAFF CLAIM MEDICAL BALANCES
===============================

Business Central Search: Employees > open the Employee Card.

- Confirm Current HR Calender is the intended active employee calendar.
- Medical Claim Balance - Self is the sum of PR Medical Ledger Entry amounts
  for Medical Category SELF, the employee number, and that HR calendar.
- Medical Claim Balance - Dependant is the corresponding DEPENDANT ledger sum.
- Do not key or hard-code balances on Staff Claims. The Staff Claims card and
  portal read these two live Employee Card FlowFields for the selected employee.
- If a balance is wrong or zero, inspect the employee's Current HR Calender and
  PR Medical Ledger Entries for SELF/DEPENDANT; correct approved setup/data only.

12. PORTAL WEB SERVICES AND SECURITY
====================================

Business Central Search: Web Services

- Confirm codeunit StaffPortal is published as StaffPortal.
- Publish page Hr Document Downloads as PgHrDownloads and query Document
  Attachments as QyDocumentAttachments for HR Policies & Forms.
- Publish query QyPortalPurchaseLines with service name QyPortalPurchaseLines.
- Confirm both endpoints answer using the portal backend service account before
  allowing users into the portal.
- Restrict SOAP/OData and the portal backend credential to the portal server;
  do not expose these endpoints or credentials to ordinary requesters.
- Give the service account only the existing portal objects and records needed
  for Purchase/Store processing and item-journal posting. Do not use SUPER in
  production. Actual Store, Procurement, Finance, and Auditor users must retain
  only their approved Business Central role permissions.
- Linked LPR, RFQ, PO, invoice, payment, GIN, and GRN numbers must refer to real
  controlled documents. The Auditor verifies them before final completion.
